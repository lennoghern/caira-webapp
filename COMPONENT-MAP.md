## Changes in macOS 27 that affect every glass surface

These belong to `GlassSurface` and `LiquidGlassProvider`, not to one component. All VERIFIED; none comes with a number.

- Glass diffuses complex content behind it more effectively (KN, SOTU).
- A darkened edge and brighter specular highlights add depth and separation (SOTU).
- A user slider runs from ultra clear to fully tinted and applies to apps that already use Liquid Glass (KN, SOTU). In macOS it sits in System Settings, Appearance, "Liquid Glass" (A27).
- Glass adapts to Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast (SOTU, HIG Materials).
- An opt-in click response makes the glass "subtly bounce". Apple says to use it on controls only (S289).
- Larger glass surfaces such as sidebars are more opaque than small ones such as toolbars (HIG Color).

No component changes tier. All 64 keep the tier from the brief, and all 64 are delivered.
